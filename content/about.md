title: About Mallory Agner
---

I'm an Athletic Communications and Public Relations professional with a passion for the intersection of sports, storytelling, digital media, and mental health advocacy. I currently serve as an Assistant Director of Athletic Communications at Manhattanville University, where I work across all 23 varsity sports to provide comprehensive coverage, create engaging digital content, manage website updates, and support athletic events and media operations. My role includes developing gameday, countdown, player focus, and team focus graphics, maintaining accurate and timely website content, and collaborating with coaches and teams throughout the academic year.

Prior to Manhattanville, I worked with James Madison University Athletic Communications, providing real-time social media updates and statistics across sports including football, field hockey, soccer, volleyball, basketball, lacrosse, baseball, and softball. Through this experience, I developed skills in live statistics, social media management, media communication, and operating DAKTRONICS and Statcrew systems.

My experience also includes working with major sporting events and collegiate athletics organizations, including the NCAA Final Four, ESPN College GameDay, the Naismith Awards, Marymount University Athletics, and George Mason University Athletics.

Alongside my work in athletics communications, I'm passionate about mental health advocacy within the student-athlete community. Through Morgan's Message, I founded the James Madison University chapter in 2024 and have continued supporting the organization through campus advising, advocacy, fundraising, and mental health-focused events. I currently serve as a campus advisor at Manhattanville, helping student-athletes build and grow their own chapter.

I hold a B.S. in Sport and Recreation Management from James Madison University and am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Sport Business Management at Manhattanville University, expected May 2027. I'm experienced with Adobe Creative Suite, Canva, Sidearm Sports, Google Suite, Microsoft Office, DAKTRONICS, Statcrew, and social media platforms including Instagram, X, Bluesky, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook.

I thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments where I can combine communication, creativity, and sports business strategy to tell meaningful stories and create engaging experiences for student-athletes, teams, and fans.
